## Paste-Ready ElevenLabs Block

--- PASTE INTO ELEVENLABS (copy everything between the lines) ---
[ominous, hushed] What if Earth suddenly stopped spinning?

[grave, deliberate] One side burns in eternal day. The other freezes in endless night.

[building tension] The atmosphere keeps moving. Supersonic winds scour the surface at a thousand miles per hour.

[urgent] Oceans surge toward the poles. Entire continents rise from the drowning depths.

[cold, clinical] The magnetic field collapses. Cosmic radiation strips the air away, molecule by molecule.

[stark] Extremes beyond survival. One hundred Celsius day. Minus one hundred Celsius night.

[quiet wonder] Only extremophiles endure. Deep underground. Deep ocean vents. Life... finds a way.

[warm, reflective] Earth stops. Life persists. What If... continues.
